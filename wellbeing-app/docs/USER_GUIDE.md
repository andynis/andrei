# User Guide - Well-being Check-in App

Complete guide for students, teachers, and administrators.

---

## For Students 👦👧

### Getting Started

#### 1. Download the App

**iPhone/iPad:**
1. Open the App Store
2. Search for "Well-being Check-in [Your School]"
3. Tap "Get" and install
4. Open the app

**Android:**
1. Open Google Play Store
2. Search for "Well-being Check-in [Your School]"
3. Tap "Install"
4. Open the app

#### 2. First-Time Setup

When you open the app for the first time:

1. **Enter Your First Name**
   - Just your first name (not your full name)
   - Example: "Emma" or "Mohamed"

2. **Enter Your Age**
   - Your current age
   - Example: "12"

3. **Consent Forms**
   ✓ My parent/guardian gave permission
   ✓ I understand the privacy policy

4. **Tap "Get Started"**

### Daily Check-ins

#### How to Do a Check-in

**Step 1: Start Check-in**
- Open the app
- Tap the big blue "Start Check-in" button

**Step 2: Scan QR Code**
- Point your phone at the QR code in your classroom
- The camera will open automatically
- Line up the QR code in the frame
- Wait for the beep ✓

**Step 3: Answer Questions** (Takes about 30 seconds!)

**Question 1: How are you feeling?**
```
😢  😞  😐  🙂  😊  😄
Tap the emoji that matches your mood
```

**Question 2: Energy Level**
```
Very tired ━━━━━ Full of energy
Tap on the colored scale
```

**Question 3: Social Comfort**
```
Uncomfortable ━━━━━ Very comfortable
How do you feel around others today?
```

**Question 4: Feeling Okay?**
```
[Yes ✓]  [No ✗]
Tap one
```

If you tap "No":
- Pick any symptoms you have:
  🤕 Headache
  🤢 Stomach ache
  😴 Very tired
  🤧 Cold/Cough
  😷 Sore throat
  😵 Dizzy
  😣 Body pain
  🩹 Other

**Question 5: Anything else? (Optional)**
```
Type here if you want to share more...
(You can skip this if you want)
```

**Step 4: Submit**
- Tap "Submit Check-in"
- See your thank you message!
- You're done! 🌟

### Important Things to Know

#### When to Check In
- ✅ Morning when you arrive
- ✅ During the school day if you want
- ⏰ Wait at least 2 hours between check-ins

#### Your Privacy
- ✅ Your answers are private
- ✅ Only your teachers can see them
- ✅ Your name is kept confidential
- ✅ It's okay to be honest!

#### Optional Participation
- ✅ You can choose not to participate
- ✅ You can skip a day if you want
- ✅ No one will be upset if you don't check in

#### If You Need Help
- 🤗 Tell your teacher if something is wrong
- 🤗 You can always talk to a trusted adult
- 🤗 The school nurse or counselor is here for you

### Frequently Asked Questions (Students)

**Q: What happens after I submit?**
A: Your teacher gets a notification. If your score shows you might need support, they may check in with you privately.

**Q: Do other students see my answers?**
A: No! Only your teachers can see your responses.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: It's okay! Just do your best. You can do another check-in later.

**Q: Do I have to answer all questions?**
A: The main questions (1-4) are required. The "anything else" part is optional.

**Q: What if I can't scan the QR code?**
A: Try:
- Moving closer/farther from the QR code
- Holding your phone steady
- Making sure there's good lighting
- Ask your teacher for help

**Q: Can I use the app at home?**
A: The app is designed for use at school. You need to scan the classroom QR code to submit a check-in.

## For Teachers 👨‍🏫👩‍🏫

### Getting Started

#### 1. Access the Dashboard

Open your web browser and go to:
```
https://wellbeing.yourschool.edu/dashboard.html
```

#### 2. Log In

Use the credentials provided by your school IT department:
- Email: your-email@school.edu
- Password: [provided securely]

**First login:** Change your password immediately!

#### 3. Set Up Your Classroom

**Create Classroom:**
1. Click "Create Classroom"
2. Enter classroom details:
   - Classroom name (e.g., "Room 205")
   - Grade level
   - Your name
   - Your email (for alerts)
3. Click "Create"

**Generate QR Code:**
1. Select your classroom from dropdown
2. Click "Generate QR Code"
3. Click "Print QR Code"
4. Post the printed QR code in your classroom

**Display Location:**
- Near the door (students scan when entering)
- Visible to all students
- Protected from damage (laminate recommended)

### Using the Dashboard

#### Home Screen Overview

**Today's Stats:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ ✅ 23 Check-ins Today       │
│ 📈 Average Score: 78/100    │
│ ⚠️  2 Students Need Attention│
```

#### Understanding Scores

**Well-being Score (0-100):**
- 80-100: Excellent (🟢 Green)
- 60-79: Good (🟡 Yellow)
- 50-59: Okay (🟠 Orange)
- 0-49: Needs Attention (🔴 Red - triggers alert)

**Calculated From:**
- Mood (30%)
- Energy Level (20%)
- Social Comfort (20%)
- Physical Health (30%)

#### Recent Alerts Section

When a student scores below 50:
```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ⚠️ Emma (Age 11)                    │
│ Score: 35/100                        │
│ Mood: Sad 😞                        │
│ Symptoms: Headache, Fatigue         │
│ Time: 9:15 AM                       │
│ ✓ Alert email sent                  │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 7-Day Trend Chart

Shows:
- Average well-being scores over time
- Patterns and trends
- Best/worst days
- Overall classroom mood

**What to Look For:**
- Sudden drops (investigate)
- Consistent low scores (systemic issue?)
- Monday blues (common pattern)
- Pre-exam stress

### Responding to Alerts

#### When You Receive an Alert Email

**Email Contains:**
- Student's first name and age
- Well-being score
- Mood and symptoms reported
- Any additional notes from student
- Timestamp

**Recommended Actions:**

**1. Immediate (Within 1 hour):**
- Discreetly check in with the student
- Ask if they're okay
- Offer support

**Example Approach:**
```
"Hi Emma, I noticed you checked in this morning.
How are you feeling? Is there anything I can help with?"
```

**2. If Serious Concern:**
- Contact school nurse
- Notify designated safeguarding lead
- Follow school safeguarding procedures
- Document the interaction

**3. Follow-up:**
- Monitor subsequent check-ins
- Continue providing support
- Update parents if needed (follow school policy)

#### Privacy Reminder
- Never discuss individual student data publicly
- Keep conversations private
- Follow GDPR and school policies

### Monitoring Patterns

#### Look for:

**Individual Patterns:**
- Student consistently scoring low
- Sudden changes in well-being
- Specific days of the week
- Correlation with events (tests, etc.)

**Classroom Patterns:**
- Whole class low energy (ventilation? timing?)
- Monday mornings particularly low
- Post-lunch dip
- Seasonal variations

**Action Steps:**
- Individual: One-on-one support
- Classroom-wide: Adjust schedule, activities, environment

### Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- Check dashboard daily
- Respond to alerts promptly
- Maintain confidentiality
- Document concerns
- Communicate with parents when appropriate
- Follow school procedures

❌ **Don't:**
- Ignore alerts
- Share student data publicly
- Use data punitively
- Make students feel surveilled
- Discuss in front of other students

### Frequently Asked Questions (Teachers)

**Q: A student scored low but seems fine. What should I do?**
A: Still check in discreetly. They may not want to show distress publicly. A simple "How are you?" can help.

**Q: How often should I check the dashboard?**
A: At least once per day. Morning is good to catch early alerts.

**Q: Can I see individual student history?**
A: Yes, click on a student's name to see their check-in history and trends.

**Q: What if the QR code gets damaged?**
A: Regenerate it from the dashboard and print a new one.

**Q: Student forgot to check in. Should I remind them?**
A: It's optional. Don't pressure students to participate.

**Q: Can I export classroom data?**
A: Yes, for your classroom only. Contact IT for exports.

**Q: Email alerts are overwhelming. Can I adjust?**
A: Yes, contact IT to adjust the threshold or frequency.

## For School Administrators 🏫

### System Administration

#### User Management

**Create Teacher Accounts:**
```bash
# Via API or database
POST /api/auth/create-user
{
  "email": "teacher@school.edu",
  "password": "temporary-password-123",
  "fullName": "Jane Smith",
  "role": "teacher",
  "schoolId": "your-school-id"
}
```

**Roles:**
- `teacher`: Classroom access only
- `admin`: Full school access
- `principal`: All classrooms, reports

#### School Setup

**1. Create School Record:**
```sql
INSERT INTO schools (name, address, contact_email)
VALUES ('Your School Name', '123 School St', 'admin@school.edu');
```

**2. Create Classrooms:**
- Assign to teachers
- Generate QR codes
- Distribute to classrooms

**3. Distribute Consent Forms:**
- Send to all parents
- Collect signed forms
- Store securely

#### Monitoring & Reports

**School-Wide Dashboard:**
- Total students registered
- Daily check-in rate
- Average well-being score
- Students needing attention
- Classroom comparison

**Generate Reports:**
```javascript
GET /api/dashboard/school/:schoolId
```

**Export Data (GDPR):**
```javascript
GET /api/dashboard/export/:appId
```

### GDPR Administration

#### Handle Data Subject Requests

**Right to Access:**
1. Verify identity
2. Export data: `GET /api/dashboard/export/:appId`
3. Provide to parent/student within 30 days

**Right to Erasure:**
1. Verify identity
2. Delete data: `DELETE /api/dashboard/student/:appId`
3. Confirm deletion

**Right to Rectification:**
1. Verify identity
2. Update database record
3. Confirm changes

#### Data Retention

**Automatic Cleanup:**
- Runs daily
- Deletes check-ins older than 1 year (configurable)
- Logs all deletions

**Manual Cleanup:**
```sql
SELECT cleanup_old_checkins();
```

### Frequently Asked Questions (Administrators)

**Q: How do we handle a data breach?**
A: Follow the incident response plan in docs/GDPR_COMPLIANCE.md. Notify DPO and authorities within 72 hours.

**Q: Can we change the alert threshold?**
A: Yes, modify `ALERT_THRESHOLD` in environment variables.

**Q: How long is data retained?**
A: Default is 1 year. Configurable via `DATA_RETENTION_DAYS`.

**Q: What if parents want data deleted?**
A: Process via right to erasure. Use DELETE endpoint or contact DPO.
