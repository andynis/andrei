# Privacy Policy - Well-being Check-in App

**Last Updated:** November 2025

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Well-being Check-in App ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects student data. This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws.

## Data Controller

The school is the data controller for all personal data collected through this App. Contact your school's Data Protection Officer for any privacy-related inquiries.

## Data We Collect

### 1. Student Information
- **First name only** (no surname)
- **Age**
- **Unique app ID** (automatically generated)
- **Registration date**

### 2. Check-in Data
- **Emotional indicators:** Mood, energy level, social comfort
- **Physical health:** Self-reported symptoms
- **Optional notes:** Text entered by student
- **Timestamp:** When check-in was submitted
- **Classroom ID:** QR code scan data
- **Well-being score:** Calculated from responses

## Legal Basis for Processing

We process student data under the following legal bases:
- **Consent:** Parental/guardian consent for students under 16
- **Legitimate Interest:** Monitoring student well-being for safeguarding purposes
- **Public Task:** Fulfilling the school's duty of care

## How We Use the Data

### Primary Purposes
1. **Student Support:** Identifying students who may need additional support
2. **Safeguarding:** Detecting potential well-being concerns
3. **Pattern Analysis:** Monitoring trends in classroom well-being
4. **Teacher Alerts:** Notifying teachers when scores fall below thresholds

### We Do NOT
- Share data with third parties (except as legally required)
- Use data for marketing purposes
- Sell student data
- Track students outside of school hours
- Require students to participate

## Data Storage and Security

### Security Measures
- **Encryption:** All data transmitted using HTTPS/TLS
- **Database Security:** Password-protected PostgreSQL database
- **Access Control:** Role-based access (teachers can only see their classrooms)
- **Audit Logs:** All data access is logged
- **Regular Backups:** Encrypted daily backups

### Storage Duration
- **Active check-ins:** Retained for 1 year (configurable)
- **Archived data:** Deleted after retention period
- **Audit logs:** Retained for 3 years
- **Student accounts:** Deleted upon graduation or withdrawal

## Student and Parent Rights (GDPR)

### Right to Access
Students/parents can request a copy of all data stored about them.

### Right to Rectification
Incorrect data can be corrected upon request.

### Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
Students/parents can request deletion of all data, except where retention is legally required.

### Right to Data Portability
Data can be exported in a machine-readable format (JSON).

### Right to Object
Students/parents can object to data processing for specific purposes.

### Right to Withdraw Consent
Consent can be withdrawn at any time without penalty.

## Parental Consent

### Requirements
- **Students under 16:** Explicit parental/guardian consent required
- **Students 16+:** Can provide their own consent
- **Consent form:** Must be signed before app registration
- **Withdrawal:** Parents can withdraw consent at any time

### Information Provided to Parents
- Purpose of data collection
- Types of data collected
- Who has access to the data
- Data retention period
- Student rights

## Data Sharing

### Within School
- **Teachers:** Can view data for their classrooms only
- **Designated Safeguarding Lead:** Can view all data
- **School Admin:** System administration access
- **School Nurse/Counselor:** May access with proper authorization

### External Sharing
Data may be shared with:
- **Authorities:** If legally required (e.g., safeguarding concerns)
- **Parents/Guardians:** Upon request for their child's data
- **External Support Services:** With explicit consent only

We do NOT share data with:
- Commercial third parties
- Marketing companies
- Social media platforms
- Other schools (without explicit consent)

## Anonymization and Aggregation

- **Aggregate Statistics:** May be used for school improvement
- **Research:** Only anonymized, non-identifiable data
- **Reports:** School-level trends without individual identification

## Automated Decision-Making

The App uses automated scoring to calculate well-being scores:
- **Threshold Alerts:** Automatically triggered for low scores
- **No High-Stakes Decisions:** Human review always required
- **Transparency:** Scoring algorithm is documented and available

## Data Breach Procedures

In the event of a data breach:
1. **Notification:** Relevant authorities notified within 72 hours
2. **Assessment:** Impact assessment conducted
3. **Affected Parties:** Parents/students informed if high risk
4. **Mitigation:** Immediate steps to prevent further breach
5. **Documentation:** Full incident report maintained

## Children's Privacy

### Special Protections
- **Age-appropriate interface:** Designed for ages 5-18
- **Limited data collection:** Only essential information
- **No profiling:** Students are not profiled or tracked
- **No behavioral advertising:** No ads or tracking cookies
- **Parental control:** Parents can review and delete data

## Cookies and Tracking

- **No tracking cookies** used in mobile app
- **Essential cookies only** on teacher dashboard
- **No analytics tracking** of individual students
- **No third-party trackers**

## International Data Transfers

- Data is stored within the EU/EEA
- No transfers to countries outside EU/EEA without adequate safeguards
- Cloud providers must be GDPR-compliant
